# ADR 0002 — Clean-room product, no reverse engineering of competitors
Status: Accepted (2026-10-19)

## Decision
No decompiling, scraping or API sniffing of MyFitnessPal or other competitors, and no use of their content or food databases.
Competitive input is limited to public marketing pages, app-store listings, press releases and
the founder's own notes and screenshots as a user. REA (reverse-engineering MCP) is out of scope.
## Why
MFP's ToS forbid reverse engineering, scraping, derivative works and use for "research and development of your own products";
the EU also protects databases with a sui-generis right. Tainted code or data would put store listings and launch at risk.
